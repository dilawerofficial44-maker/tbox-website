The product

A physical product with massive traction — and a digital layer that had to match it.

BetterBack is not a software product. It's a posture correction device — a physical strap that wraps around the lower back and knees, rotating the pelvis into its natural alignment and eliminating the root cause of most sitting-related back pain. It was created by Katherine Krug after a year of debilitating sciatica and herniated disc pain left her unable to sit at all.

The device raised $1.65M on Kickstarter from 16,500 backers, appeared on Shark Tank, and secured a deal with Barbara Corcoran. By the time TBox was engaged, BetterBack had extraordinary product-market fit and 4.7-star reviews — but the digital commerce layer needed to be rebuilt to match the credibility and scale of the brand.

What TBox built

Every layer of the digital commerce stack — end to end

TBox handled the complete buildout of BetterBack's e-commerce platform, from Shopify theme development to email marketing flows to performance analytics. The goal wasn't just a storefront — it was a conversion engine tuned for a product with a compelling story, strong social proof, and a wide audience.

01

Shopify store setup & custom theme development

Configured the Shopify store from the ground up and customized the Zest theme to match BetterBack's brand voice — clean, clinical, and credible. Every section was tailored: hero layout, feature breakdown, social proof, comparison tables, and checkout flow. No off-the-shelf template dropped in without thought.

02

Conversion-focused product page design

The product page does more than show a product — it walks a skeptical customer through the mechanism, the proof, and the risk reversal. We designed the information hierarchy around how people actually read a landing page: hook, credibility, mechanism, social proof, and offer. The result is a page that converts browsers into buyers without requiring a sales team.

03

Bundle pricing & discount logic

Implemented multi-product bundle options (BetterBack, BetterBack + SuperStraps, 2-pack) with tiered discount logic — up to 47% off on the highest-value bundle. The pricing structure is designed to increase average order value while making the entry price feel accessible.

04

Klaviyo email & SMS marketing flows

Set up the full Klaviyo lifecycle: welcome series for new subscribers, abandoned cart recovery, post-purchase onboarding (how to use BetterBack on day one, week one, month one), re-engagement campaigns, and SMS flows for high-intent buyers. Each flow is built around the specific moments where BetterBack customers need reassurance or a nudge.

05

Review system integration (Loox + Stamped.io)

Integrated Loox for photo reviews — critical for a physical product where customers want to see it in use on real people — and Stamped.io for structured text reviews, star ratings, and aggregate widgets. 8,400+ reviews is a significant asset; the integration makes sure that trust is visible at every decision point in the funnel.

06

Checkout optimisation & Shop Pay

Configured Shop Pay accelerated checkout and the 60-day money-back trial flow — a powerful risk-reversal mechanism that removes the final objection for fence-sitters. The returns/exchange portal (Redo) was also integrated to handle post-purchase without burdening customer support.

07

Analytics & ad tracking setup

Implemented Facebook Pixel for Meta Ads retargeting, Google Analytics for storefront behaviour tracking, and event tagging across the key funnel steps — product view, add to cart, checkout initiation, and purchase. Every dollar spent on ads can be traced to a conversion.

08

Landing page copywriting & content strategy

Wrote the conversion copy for the product page — from the hook ("Your back isn't broken. Your chair is.") to the mechanism explanation, competitor comparison, and call to action. Copywriting for a physical health product requires making a clinical claim feel human. Every word earns its place.
