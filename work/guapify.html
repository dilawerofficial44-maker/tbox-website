The product

A platform where money is discussed openly — built for a community that needed a seat at the table.

Guapify is a financial literacy and community platform founded by CJ Harris — a media executive whose career spanned HBO, BET, and CNBC. His insight was direct: financial understanding shouldn't be gated behind advisors and jargon. The Black community, in particular, deserves a space where wealth-building conversations happen in plain language, through trusted voices, with real tools.

The site brings together live market data, curated financial news, educational resources, grant discovery, a members-only paid community, and a direct connection to CJ's voice and perspective — all under one roof. It is content platform, community hub, and financial education resource in one.

What TBox built

Every feature on the platform — built from scratch and maintained over time

TBox handled the full build of Guapify from nothing — architecture decisions, feature implementation, design, and ongoing development. The platform required a broader technical footprint than a typical WordPress site, integrating live financial data, community infrastructure, payment handling, content feeds, and media embeds into a cohesive experience.

01

WordPress platform build & custom theme

Architected and built the site on WordPress with Elementor for flexible page composition. The dark-themed design reflects Guapify's premium positioning — clean typography, strong contrast, and a layout that works across devices. LiteSpeed Cache was configured for performance, keeping page loads fast despite the content-heavy structure.

02

Live stock market ticker

Integrated TradingView's widget to display a curated live ticker of 34 stocks — including Black-owned companies (Urban One/UONE, Broadway Financial/BYFC, Carver Bancorp/CARV) alongside major consumer brands. The selection was deliberate: stocks the community recognises and has a stake in, not a generic S&P feed.

03

Paid community & membership (Mighty Networks)

Integrated the Mighty Networks (mn.co) community platform to power Guapify's paid membership — where subscribers can join discussions, access exclusive content, and participate in the community CJ has built. The integration handles authentication, subscription state, and routing between the public site and the gated community seamlessly.

04

Guap News — content aggregation engine

Built a news aggregation system that pulls Black business, culture, and finance articles from external sources and surfaces them in the "Guap News" section. The feed is curated and rendered in a card layout, with links back to original publishers. A separate Resources section applies the same pattern to grants, tools, and financial resources for Black entrepreneurs.

05

Mortgage calculator tool

Built a custom mortgage calculator — a practical tool that lets users input total loan amount, down payment, interest rate, and amortization period to estimate monthly payments. Financial tools like this turn the site from an information resource into something users return to for active decision-making.

06

Video & YouTube integration

Embedded YouTube video content on the homepage and throughout the site, supporting CJ's content-creator presence on his channel. Video is central to how Guapify builds trust — letting the founder speak directly to the community without requiring a separate platform.

07

Social sharing & multi-platform presence

Integrated social sharing across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Threads — allowing content to flow from the platform to social, and driving community members back to the site. The social layer is woven into the header, footer, and CTA sections rather than treated as an afterthought.

08

SEO & Google Ads infrastructure

Set up the technical SEO foundation — structured metadata, canonical tags, sitemap, and clean URL architecture — along with Google Ads tracking. The content-heavy nature of the site (regular news articles and resources) gives Guapify strong organic crawl surface that compounds over time.

09

Ongoing maintenance & feature additions

The relationship with Guapify didn't end at launch. TBox continues to support the platform — maintaining uptime, adding new features as CJ's vision evolves, updating content structures, and expanding the platform's capabilities. This is a long-term partnership, not a handoff.

Technical breakdown

A production stack with more moving parts than it looks like from the outside

Feature Implementation
Platform WordPress with Elementor page builder. Custom theme and component library built around Guapify's brand identity — dark palette, green accents, high-contrast typography.
Performance LiteSpeed Cache with server-level caching, JS/CSS minification, lazy-loaded images, and CDN asset delivery for fast global page loads.
Stock ticker TradingView widget displaying 34 curated stocks including Black-owned public companies (UONE, BYFC, CARV) and major brands the community engages with daily.
Community & membership Mighty Networks (guapify.mn.co) — paid membership community with sign-in flow integrated into the main site's navigation. Handles subscription state and routes authenticated users to the gated community.
News aggregation RSS/feed-based content aggregation pulling Black business and finance articles into a curated "Guap News" section with card layout, source attribution, and external links.
Financial tools Custom mortgage calculator with inputs for loan amount, down payment, interest rate, and amortization period — converting the site into an active decision-support tool.
Video YouTube video embeds integrated on the homepage and content sections, supporting CJ's video content strategy without requiring a separate platform.
Social integration Multi-platform social sharing and follow links across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Threads — woven into site navigation, section footers, and CTAs.
SEO infrastructure Full technical SEO setup — structured metadata, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, and clean permalink architecture. Google Ads conversion tracking integrated.
Resources section Aggregated grants, funding opportunities, and financial tools for Black entrepreneurs — curated and surfaced alongside editorial content using the same feed-based architecture as Guap News.
