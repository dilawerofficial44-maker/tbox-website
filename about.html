Our story

A decade of building things that last

TBox started in 2014 with a straightforward conviction: engineering quality compounds. A well-built first version is cheaper than a poorly-built one at the tenth iteration. We set out to be the studio that got it right the first time.

The early years were mobile-first — iOS and Android products for clients across the Netherlands, the UAE, and North America. We built subscriber-facing apps, field service platforms, and e-commerce experiences that needed to perform in real conditions, not just demos.

As the product scope of our clients grew, so did our capability. Full-stack web, SaaS architecture, payment integrations, real-time systems — the team expanded and the engagements deepened. Several of our early clients are still clients today, because we stayed with them through growth, not just launch.

When AI tooling reached a threshold of genuine usefulness, we embedded it into how we work — not as a marketing story, but as a practical multiplier. Our engineers are senior enough to know when AI output is right, wrong, or dangerously close to wrong. That judgment is what separates AI-augmented engineering from AI-generated noise.

Founder

Dilawer Hussain

Founder & CEO — 16 years in FinTech, SaaS & AI Product

Dilawer started TBox with a single belief: the best engineering teams in the world treat software as a craft, not a commodity. Over sixteen years working across FinTech, SaaS, and mobile products, he developed a sharp sense for where engineering quality compounds and where it doesn't.

He built TBox around that sense — recruiting engineers who take ownership of outcomes, not just tickets, and establishing a culture where no project ships to a standard he wouldn't personally stand behind.

The shift to AI-augmented engineering came from the same pragmatism. When AI tooling genuinely improved what senior engineers could deliver in a given sprint, TBox adopted it. Not as a brand story — as a working practice. The result is a studio where experienced engineers move faster without sacrificing the rigour that makes software last.

What we stand for

Four things that don't change engagement to engagement

01 AI-first, not AI-only
Agents amplify our engineers — they don't replace them. Every AI output is reviewed by someone who has shipped the same type of system before. Quality is non-negotiable.
02 Speed without shortcuts
We ship fast because we know what to build — not because we skip tests, skip reviews, or defer the hard architectural decisions to the next sprint.
03 Senior all the way
No layers. No handoffs to juniors. The engineers you meet at the start of a project are the engineers who build it. Every one of them has shipped production systems at scale.
04 Radical transparency
You see every commit, every decision, every tradeoff — in real time. If something isn't working, we tell you before it becomes a problem. No surprises at launch.
