01 Introduction

TBox Solutionz SMC Pvt Ltd (“TBox,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website at tboxsolutionz.com. We are an AI-native engineering studio based in Lahore, Pakistan, working with founders and businesses across the United States, Europe, and the Middle East.

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and what choices you have. We have written it in plain language because we believe transparency is not a legal formality — it is a professional standard.

If you have questions this document does not answer, email us at info@tboxsolutionz.com.

02 Information We Collect

We collect two types of information: what you give us directly, and what our analytics tool observes automatically.

Information you provide directly

When you fill out our contact form, you provide your name, email address, company name, and a description of your project. We use this to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to a mailing list or share this information with third parties for their own purposes.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, Google Analytics 4 collects anonymised usage data — only if you have given consent. This includes pages visited, time spent, your approximate location (country or city level), device type, and browser. This data does not identify you personally.

03 Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use our website. This helps us improve the site, understand which content is most useful, and make better decisions about where to invest our time.

Consent required. We do not load Google Analytics until you explicitly accept analytics cookies via the consent banner. If you decline, Google Analytics is never initialised during your visit, and no data is sent to Google.

What GA4 collects (with your consent):

  • Pages and sections you visit, and how long you spend on them
  • How you arrived at the site (direct, search, referral)
  • Device type, operating system, and browser
  • Approximate geographic location (country and city — not your precise address)
  • Interactions such as clicking buttons or navigating between pages

What GA4 does not collect: your name, email address, IP address (IP anonymisation is enabled), or any information that identifies you as an individual.

Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC under their own privacy terms. For more detail, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

04 Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. We use them only for analytics, and only with your consent.

Cookie Purpose Duration Set by

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking of any kind. The only cookies this site sets beyond your consent preference are Google Analytics cookies, and those are only set after you explicitly accept.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser’s cookies and declining when the banner reappears. You can also block cookies entirely in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use this website.

05 How We Use Information

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not use your contact details for cold outreach, newsletters, or any purpose beyond the conversation you initiated.

Analytics data (when consented) is used to:

  • Understand which pages and articles are most useful to visitors
  • Identify where the website can be improved
  • Measure the performance of our content over time

We do not use any data from this website for advertising, retargeting, or building profiles on individuals.

06 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Full stop.

The only third party that receives data from this website is Google, through Google Analytics, and only when you have given consent. Google processes this data under their own terms as a data processor on our behalf.

We may disclose information if required by law, but we have no reason to anticipate that and would notify you where legally permitted if it arose.

07 Data Retention

Contact form submissions are kept in our email inbox and used only to manage the enquiry. We do not store them in a separate database.

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default within our GA4 property, after which it is automatically deleted by Google.

Your cookie consent preference is stored in your browser’s local storage for 12 months. After that period, we will ask for your preference again.

08 Your Rights

If you are located in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with data protection legislation, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal data from our records
  • Object to how we process your data
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@tboxsolutionz.com. We will respond within 30 days. Because we collect very little data, most requests can be resolved quickly.

If you are in the EU and feel your rights have not been respected, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

09 Security

This website is served over HTTPS. Contact form submissions are transmitted securely directly to our inbox.

We do not store payment information, passwords, or sensitive personal data on this website. The data we handle is limited and low-risk by design — a deliberate choice, not an afterthought.
